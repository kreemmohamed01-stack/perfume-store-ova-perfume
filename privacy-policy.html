Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains what customer information may be collected through Ova Store, how it is used, and how we handle communication and order-related details responsibly.

Information We Collect

When a customer places an order, we may collect information such as name, email address, phone number, shipping address, city, country, notes, selected products, and chosen payment method. This information is used only for order handling and customer communication.

How We Use Your Information

Your details are used to process orders, confirm delivery information, review payment details, answer support questions, and communicate with you regarding your order status. We do not collect more information than needed for the shopping experience and order fulfillment.

Payment & Order Communication

If you choose a wallet payment or another manual confirmation method, the information you provide may be used to verify the transaction and complete your order. Order details may also be shared through the selected communication channel for confirmation and follow-up.

Data Protection

We aim to handle customer information carefully and only within the scope of order management and service improvement. While no online method is completely risk-free, Ova Store takes reasonable steps to limit unnecessary exposure of customer data.

Third-Party Services

Some parts of the customer journey may use external services such as messaging platforms, hosting, maps, or payment-related tools. These services may have their own privacy practices, and customers should review them separately when needed.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated whenever Ova Store changes its ordering process, communication methods, or website features. The latest version published on the website is the one that applies.
